# My story and discovery frame

**Ayo Ahmed — Independent concept**

> Unofficial concept. All accounts/events are synthetic. No live CRM, outreach, payments or rides.

## Thirty-second story

I chose one narrow part of a broad GTM mandate: getting a synthetic company-pay account to its first completed order. My workbench shows what is ready, what is blocked, and why an operator would choose the next action. I keep recorded extraction output separate from deterministic decisions and human confirmation. I then show a fixed-cohort experiment view and executable failure checks. The result is a reviewable concept for discussing architecture, evaluation, market variation, and commercial measurement. It is not evidence of production deployment or business lift.

## Explain it like I am five

I have pretend companies that want their employees to take a work ride. Before the pretend ride can happen, the right employee needs to be ready and the company needs the right settings. My screen is a checklist with reasons. It can suggest the next pretend step, but a person can see the reason and decide. I keep a notebook of every step, and I count every company I started with when comparing groups—even the ones that got stuck.

## Jobs to be done

| Situation | Job I want to support | Outcome I would test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I inherit an unfamiliar company-pay account | Help me see trusted setup evidence and what is still unknown | Less time spent reconstructing context; accurate blocker identification |
| I need to choose a next action | Show a bounded recommendation and its reason | Correct action selection without unsafe automatic progression |
| A member is Active but the account has no order | Help me distinguish member readiness from account activation | Fewer incorrect “activated” labels |
| A market uses a guided or assisted process | Let me adapt the operating path while keeping shared event definitions | Local adoption without losing comparability |
| I report progress to a market lead | Show outcome counts, observation windows, exposure and caveats | Decisions that do not overstate business impact |
| An extraction candidate is uncertain or invalid | Preserve the evidence and give me a review path | Graceful degradation instead of silent acceptance |

These are design hypotheses. I have not interviewed Bolt operators or observed their existing tools.

## Five Whys: a hypothesis chain to validate

1. **Why explore activation?** I want to connect a bounded workflow to the role's time-to-first-order and activation outcomes.
2. **Why inspect readiness?** My hypothesis is that an operator needs reliable setup evidence before selecting a useful next action.
3. **Why make reasons visible?** An opaque recommendation would be harder to verify, correct, or adopt across different operating processes.
4. **Why separate extraction from routing?** A plausible extraction can still be wrong or contain hostile instructions; validated fields should feed a bounded rule rather than grant permission to act.
5. **Why preserve experiment integrity?** Excluding failures or changing denominators could make an intervention appear successful without supporting an honest commercial conclusion.

I would test each link through authorised discovery and baseline instrumentation. This chain does not establish a root cause at Bolt.

## My assumptions register

| Assumption | Basis and uncertainty | How I would validate it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company-pay work-ride setup is a useful proof boundary | Public support describes relevant member, group, policy, and payment concepts | Review scope with product and operations owners |
| Three operating profiles help reveal variation | Synthetic UK/en self-service, Lithuania/lt guided, Kenya/en assisted examples only | Map actual market workflows, languages, available services, and owners |
| An operator can confirm a recommendation | Hypothetical workflow choice; no internal authority model observed | Interview operators and review permissions |
| A first completed order is a useful activation proxy | My concept definition, not an official product definition | Agree analytical event, cancellations, eligibility, and outcome window |
| Extraction will need abstention and review | General reliability design requirement; no live model is present | Evaluate a versioned model on authorised representative examples |
| Workflow improvements can influence commercial outcomes | Plausible hypothesis; no measured causal effect here | Run a preregistered pilot with adequate sample and guardrails |

## Discovery I would do before a live pilot

I would map the inherited funnel, event ownership, current automations, exceptions, and local operating practices with market leads and reps. I would ask where data arrives late, which steps actually need human judgement, and which automations are already trusted. I would join those observations to audited baseline data rather than infer a problem from a single anecdote. I would agree company-pay eligibility, permissions, privacy basis, retention, and stop conditions before requesting production access.

I would compare operator time, error and retry rates, unresolved cases, and activation windows by market and account segment. I would retain negative findings: if the main delay is outside setup, this workbench may be the wrong intervention. I would then choose what to keep, rebuild, or retire using the [roadmap and impact/effort memo](roadmap-and-priorities.md).
